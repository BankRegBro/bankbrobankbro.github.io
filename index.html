About the Author

Joey Samowitz

The analysis and interactive work on this site is written and produced by Joey Samowitz, a financial services policy professional with nine years of experience at the intersection of state and federal bank regulation.

Joey is Senior Director of Policy Development at a leading state financial regulatory organization, where he leads a team crafting policy positions that directly inform action by state regulators across the country. He formerly represented state regulators on the FSOC digital assets working group.

His writing spans bank M&A reform, AML/CFT program design, payment stablecoin architecture, model risk governance, AI in supervised institutions, and the structural tensions of the dual banking system.
